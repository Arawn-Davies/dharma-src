Shasta Abbey mirrored resources
================================

These files are unchanged local copies of Shasta Abbey publication PDFs.

Source page:
https://shastaabbey.org/publications/

Shasta Abbey states that Shasta Abbey Press publications, with noted
exceptions, are offered in the spirit of dana for free distribution, and that
copies may be downloaded, printed, and distributed so long as no changes are
made to the original.

The app keeps the original source URL and attribution on each resource page.
